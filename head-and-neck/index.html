One team. Every aspect of care.

Modeled on the Harvard Otolaryngology newsletter tradition — a single place to follow what the Head and Neck Cancer Program is doing, learning and building.

Clinical advances

New techniques, expanded service lines and care pathways — reported plainly, so referring physicians and patients can see what has changed and why it matters.

Ongoing research

Studies, trials and findings from across Cedars-Sinai, connected to the clinic. Research presented alongside the care it is meant to improve.

Integrated care

Surgery, oncology, radiology, pathology, speech, nutrition and survivorship — not separate silos, but one interdisciplinary team around each patient.
